// Who We Are

Engineering-grade fabrication, finished by hand.

High Class Steel Craft is a full-service fabrication workshop handling everything from heavy structural steel — columns, trusses and platforms — to architectural metalwork like staircases, gates, railings and custom furniture frames.

Every job moves through our shop on a tight process: design sign-off, material cutting, jig-assembly, welding, grinding and a powder-coat or paint finish — then site installation by our own crew.
